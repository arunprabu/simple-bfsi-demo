# Simple Bank

## Project requirement: Fund Transfer

Customers should be able to transfer money from their savings account to
another Simple Bank account using the bank's web app.

### Transfer rules

- The transfer amount must be greater than Rs 0.
- The sender must have sufficient balance.
- A customer may transfer at most Rs 1,00,000 per day.
- The sender and recipient accounts must be different.
- Both accounts must be active.
- Each transfer must be saved with a unique transaction ID.
- The customer should receive a quick response.

See [the detailed fund transfer requirements](docs/requirements-fund-transfer.md).
